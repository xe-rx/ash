# Surface brief: Ash idle screen

Scope: the whole 1024x600 idle screen (app/ash/view, palette in app/ash/core). Mode: experience. Read up close at the thermostat (PO, 2026-11-04).
Job: see the time, the sun's place in the day and the next prayer, as a calm picture.
Keep: desert, dune and family shapes; the gradient sky (PO chose it 2026-11-04); the sun on its invisible arc; no animation.
PO called dated: the cream table card, clock and fonts, overall flatness, the dusty colours.

## Direction contract
Seed key: cf3813cb (assigned). Chosen sketch: Sadu, the woven tent divider.

THESIS: The prayer table is woven into the Bedouin tent, a full-height sadu band on the right edge, not a rounded card floating on the picture. Refuses the frosted or cream widget card.

OWN-WORLD: Goat-hair black weave ground, undyed wool cream, madder red, saffron and indigo dyes. Pattern bands of small diamonds and stripes, a fringe at the bottom edge. Flat, no shadows, no rounded corners. The landscape and sky take the same dye family: deeper, saturated, not dusty pastel.

STORY: At a glance: the time, how long until the next prayer, and which prayers have passed, inside one desert evening.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Large cream clock left; countdown with the prayer name as a woven cream tag; the black band right, full height, Hijri date woven at the top, five rows, next prayer a madder-red row, passed prayers faded.

SIGNATURE: The weave as one grid: every row, band and gap is a multiple of one pick unit. Red means "now" only. Night is an authored dimmed weave.

RISK: Pattern bands cost items; they ship as one small deterministic PNG strip. Contrast of faded rows must still meet 4.5:1 (large text 3:1).
