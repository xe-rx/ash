# Kickoff question bank

Questions that change the architecture or the plan, collected from real runs where they were asked too late. Ask the ones the idea leaves open; skip the ones the PO already answered. Ask one at a time, with your recommended answer first.

## Purpose and users
- Who is the first user, concretely? What do they do today instead?
- What does success look like in numbers (users, latency, accuracy, cost)? How will we measure it?
- What is the first thing the PO wants to show someone?

## Scope
- What is explicitly out of scope for the first milestone? What is "a future feature" (record it, don't build it)?
- One region, language, platform or dataset first, or all at once? (Start with one.)
- Which languages does the UI need? (Each extra one is a permanent cost.)

## Commercial status and licences
- Will this ever be commercial? (It decides which data, models and libraries are allowed.)
- Which licence for the code, and which for generated outputs and data? One licence for all outputs is simplest.
- Whose attribution must we show, and where?

## Privacy and data
- What personal data is involved (location, accounts, analytics)? Must any of it stay on the device?
- What data do we store, how large is it (measure a sample), where does it live, and who pays for it?
- Is any data generated by AI? If so: run once and store, or generate on demand? How is AI output marked and quality-gated, and what is the fallback if it fails the gate?

## Platforms and delivery
- Web, PWA, native, desktop, CLI? Offline? Which devices must it run well on?
- Where is it hosted, and what is the monthly ceiling? Is near-zero hosting (static files) possible?
- Does any heavy job (data build, training, crawl) need to run? On whose machine, for how long, and does that machine sleep?

## Third parties
- Which external APIs and data providers do we depend on? What are their terms, rate limits and identification rules?
- Do we need anyone's permission? (Drafts go to the outbox; the PO sends them.)

## Quality bars
- What accuracy, performance or accessibility bar must the first milestone meet, and how is it checked?
- What may be approximate, and how is that shown to the user honestly?

## Authority and process
- Who has final authority on content or domain questions until an expert joins?
- What may the team do without asking (merge to main, run long jobs, spend money, contact people, push to a remote)? What always needs the PO?
- How often does the PO want to see progress, and in what form (live link, deck, short report)?

## Compute and cost
- Is there a budget for compute or APIs? Express it in money and in hardware, not in tokens.
- What is the stop rule if a costly approach fails its quality gate?
