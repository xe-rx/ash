# Risk register: Ash

From the kickoff pre-mortem (written by the tech lead from the prep reports); reviewed at the end of every round by the tech lead.

| # | Risk (why the project failed) | Likelihood | Impact | Owner role | Mitigation | Early signal | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | The dim hook doesn't work on the PO's firmware, so Ash is never truly ambient | M | H | frontend-engineer | **Top 3.** Round 0 probe; test the hook on the device early in round 2; agreed fallback is tile-opened until the PO decides | The tile's visibility doesn't change during dim, or the hook needs a firmware file | open |
| R2 | An install or a hung Ash leaves the thermostat screen unusable, and the household loses trust in it | L | H | devops-engineer | **Top 3.** Own directory only; staged install with symlink swap; guard auto-rollback unless confirmed; bounded core loops; rehearse GUI death and rollback before the soak | `qt-gui` PID changes after install; the guard rolls back; heating doesn't survive the rehearsal | open |
| R3 | Times are wrong or disagree with the mosque, so the family stops trusting the screen | M | H | test-engineer | **Top 3.** Golden tests against `adhan-js` across a full year, within a minute; the PO compares a few dates with their mosque before the soak; config offsets only by PO decision | Any golden test over a minute; the PO reports a mismatch | open |
| R4 | Date edges break: Isha after midnight, daylight-saving changes, or a wrong device clock or timezone | M | H | frontend-engineer | Tests for Isha rollover and both DST days; `offsetAt(ms)` from the view; the probe records the Toon's clock and timezone; toonsim reports a JS `Date` DST issue, so check on the device | Next prayer or countdown is wrong near midnight or on DST days; `date` differs from local time | open |
| R5 | We can't reproduce the Toon off-device, so bugs show up only on the wall and each loop needs the PO | H | M | devops-engineer | Local toolchain is PyQt5 5.11.3, which bundles Qt 5.11.2, and renders offscreen with the software backend (measured 2026-10-31); probe confirms the Toon's version; try `toonsim`; keep adapters thin; QML kept to QtQuick primitives | Qt 5.11 won't install in WSL or CI; a screenshot differs from the PO's photo; a QML import is missing on the device | open |
| R6 | Home location or household details leak via git, logs or shared screenshots | L | H | security-reviewer | ADR 0003; example config only; coordinate and network checks in CI; no config values in logs; the PO checks device output and photos before sharing | The coordinate check fires; a log line contains config values | open |
| R7 | A firmware update removes or breaks Ash | M | M | devops-engineer | Firmware version check in preflight; reinstall steps in `docs/INSTALL.md`; no firmware edits, so the stock screen returns | The tile disappears after an update; preflight reports a different version | open |
| R8 | Screen burn-in, or the display is too bright at night | M | M | frontend-engineer | Plus or minus 4 px scene shift; dark night palette; leave brightness to firmware unless the probe shows control is available | The PO reports a ghost image or a bright hallway | open |
| R9 | Performance is too heavy for the device, and the whole thermostat UI lags | M | M | frontend-engineer | Primitives only; one minute timer stopped when hidden; PO records `top` during the soak; PNG layers as the escape hatch | CPU stays high while idle; touch feels slow | open |
| R10 | The scene looks clip-art or culturally off, so the family doesn't want it on the wall | M | H | frontend-engineer | Faceless simple shapes; show the PO a static screenshot in round 1 before building more scene; the PO has final say | The PO asks for repeated redraws | open |
| R11 | The Toon has no usable audio, so the chime can't work | H | L | researcher | Probe checks audio; first in the agreed cut order; drop it without affecting anything else | No `/dev/snd` or player on the device | open |
| R12 | The PO's time on device loops becomes the bottleneck | M | M | tech lead | Batch device requests per round; one-command install, rollback and report; test all we can off-device | A round is blocked waiting on a device step | open |
